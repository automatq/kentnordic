The 5 Nights South Coast extends our best-selling southern loop with an extra
day on the road, reaching east to the wave-battered peaks of Vestrahorn at
Stokksnes and lingering longer among the glaciers, canyons and waterfalls of
the south. It suits agencies whose clients want the headline sights at an
unhurried pace, with the Golden Circle and a Blue Lagoon soak saved for the
journey back to Reykjavik.
