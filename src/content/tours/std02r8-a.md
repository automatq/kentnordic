The 8 Nights Round Iceland (Anti-Clockwise) laps the entire Ring Road in the
reverse direction — heading down the South Coast on day one and returning via
Snaefellsnes and the Golden Circle. Running the loop anti-clockwise front-loads
the glacier lagoon and East Fjords while the light is freshest, and gives
agencies a natural counterpart to the clockwise departure for back-to-back or
alternating group dates. Every night is hotel-based and every day fully guided
by a licensed driver-guide.
