The 6 Nights South Coast & Snaefellsnes broadens our bestselling South Coast
route with a full day on the Snaefellsnes Peninsula — a compact showcase of
everything that makes Iceland extraordinary, from seal beaches and black churches
to the photogenic Kirkjufell. It is an ideal step up for agencies whose clients
want more than the standard highlights loop without committing to a full
round-Iceland itinerary, all fully guided and comfortably paced from Reykjavik.
