The 7 Nights Round Iceland is our flagship circuit — a full lap of the Ring Road
that strings together the north's geothermal wonders, the wild East Fjords, the
glacier lagoon and the classic South Coast in one continuous, fully guided loop.
It suits agencies whose clients want to see the whole island rather than a single
corner, with a licensed driver-guide, comfortable 4*/3* hotels and a steady,
well-judged pace from Keflavik back to Reykjavik.
