Ring Road covered clockwise at an unhurried pace, with a licensed driver-guide
throughout. It suits agencies whose clients want to see every side of the island
in one seamless loop, from the Snaefellsnes peninsula and the volcanic north to
the remote East Fjords and the glacier country of the south. Comfortable 4* and
3* hotels and daily breakfasts keep the long-distance touring relaxed from
arrival to departure.
